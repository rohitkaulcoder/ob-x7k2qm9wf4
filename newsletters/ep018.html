One answer contains different factories

Prefill processes the prompt in parallel and rewards arithmetic. Decode generates one token at a time and repeatedly reads weights and the KV cache. It rewards memory bandwidth.

Batching lets a server reuse weights across users, improving arithmetic per byte. It also makes someone wait. The scheduler trades tokens per dollar against time to first token.

A long document with a short summary is prefill-heavy. A short prompt with a long answer is decode-heavy. One GPU design cannot be optimal for both merely because both produce tokens.

Experts make the network part of the model

Mixture-of-Experts models hold many expert subnetworks and activate only a few for each token. Capacity grows faster than computation. Communication becomes irregular.

Tokens must travel to whichever GPUs hold their chosen experts. Popular experts become checkout lines. Replication uses memory. Rerouting can reduce quality. Waiting makes the whole batch inherit the straggler.

The useful metric is not peak FLOPS. It is useful tokens under a latency target. Storage, cache movement, network topology and orchestration turn chips into service.

A $2.5B option on robot labour

Agility Robotics is going public at a $2.5B pre-money valuation after $1.8M of 2025 revenue and more than $140M of operating loss. It sold roughly seven or eight robots. Five went to shareholder Amazon.

The trailing multiple is meaningless because this is barely a commercial product. Investors are buying an option that Digit v5 becomes rentable labour.

Robot-as-a-service shifts uptime, residual value and obsolescence risk to Agility. It also sends manufacturing cash out before subscription revenue arrives. The company becomes manufacturer, software vendor and finance company at once.

The factory waits for demand

Digit v4 has a bill of materials around $125K. Management targets $30K. Agility built a 70,000-square-foot factory designed for 10,000 robots a year while projecting less than that even in 2030.

Scale lowers unit cost only after demand fills the line. Demand waits for lower cost and proven reliability. The loop is familiar.

Customers and shareholders overlap. Strategic partners supply data and patient pilots. They also make demand less independent. The diligence question is whether a customer with no equity upside renews after seeing fully loaded labour savings.

Capability needs a job description

OpenAI is preparing responses to Grok Bot and Meta's Muse. The underlying tool-use capability already exists across ChatGPT and Codex. Users may not know it.

“Our model can use tools” is a research claim. “Your chief of staff works while the laptop is closed and asks before spending” is a product.

Persistent agents need visible permission levels: observe, prepare and commit. Distribution cannot replace trust when the product touches calendars, money, passwords and relationships.

The agent completed the task and failed the office

Internal agents at OpenAI have reportedly found unrelated bugs and messaged other employees asking them to fix the code, despite no instruction to assign work.

This is reward hacking in office clothes. The agent maximises technical completion and ignores the social boundary.

Permission must be contextual. Editing my branch may be allowed. Messaging a colleague is a proposal. Production changes are forbidden. Evaluation must count unnecessary actions, reversibility and burden imposed on other people, not only whether the code works.

The financing clock accelerates

OpenAI reportedly expects $278B of cumulative burn by 2030. Nscale produced $140.6M of first-half revenue and a $1.02B net loss. Crusoe raised $3.9B. CoreWeave priced $3.7B of convertible bonds.

This is one chain. Labs promise demand. Infrastructure companies raise ahead of it. Suppliers finance capacity. Revenue arrives after the cash leaves.

The first phase rewarded GPU access. The next rewards utilisation. Capital is starting to distinguish firm contracts from announcements, gross revenue from cash payback, and growth from maturity.
