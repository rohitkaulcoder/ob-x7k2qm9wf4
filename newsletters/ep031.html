Leverage is a ratio, and the ratio is a promise

Paramount Skydance is due to close its $110 billion purchase of Warner Bros. Discovery on Tuesday. The combined company, to be called Skydance, carries $79 billion of debt against about $12 billion of annual EBITDA. EBITDA is cash earnings before interest, tax and asset wear, so the ratio asks how many years of that paycheck it takes to repay what is owed. $79B / $12B is about 6.6 times.

David Ellison's case rests on $6 billion of synergies, which means cost cuts. If they arrive, EBITDA becomes $18 billion and the ratio falls to about 4.4 times. Same debt, different denominator. Most of it, over $50 billion of bonds and term loans, was raised last week at 6.3% to 9.125%. As an illustration only, $50B at those rates is roughly $3.2B to $4.6B of interest a year.

The market was not generous. Bloomberg reported the new bonds fell once they traded, leaving holders with more than $100 million in losses, and Paramount stock is down 29% since the deal was announced in February. WBD's own filings project cable revenue down 8% and cable profit down 30% over four years, a case of operating leverage in reverse. Larry Ellison has pledged over a third of his 38% Oracle stake for loans, and Oracle is down 27% this year.

Source: The Information, The Briefing (Martin Peers), Oct 4, 2026. Paywalled for readers. Ratios and interest ranges are our arithmetic.

India PE-VC, nine months in: bigger cheques, thinner seed

January to September: $21.6 billion across 602 deals. Q1 was $7.7B over 248 deals, Q2 $5.2B over 175, Q3 $8.6B, driven by large private equity cheques rather than broad activity.

Seed fell 57% in Q3, to $255M from $594M in Q2. Series A ran $528M, $199M and $365M across the three quarters. Financial services including fintech dropped to $334M in Q3 from $1.4B in Q2, while healthcare ($2.28B) and energy and climate ($2.24B) led. Enterprise tech kept sliding, $854M, $532M, then $292M.

Read together: money goes where it can be deployed in size with visible cash flows. Simple Energy's Rs 1,750 crore Series C, led by the Thyrocare founder's family office, is the same pattern.

Source: The Runway, Edition 50, Oct 4, 2026.

Moneyview's 62% pop, and what the number means

Moneyview listed at Rs 55 against a Rs 34 issue price, a 61.7% premium (21 / 34), after being subscribed 98.46 times. Oversubscription means bids were about 98 times the shares offered, so allocations get scaled down.

The Rs 1,091.68 crore offer included a Rs 341.68 crore offer for sale, which means about Rs 750 crore was fresh issue that the company keeps. Sellers included Accel, Tiger Global and Ribbit. FY26 revenue from operations was Rs 3,351.6 crore with profit after tax of Rs 242.71 crore. Other fintech listings this year were softer: Aye Finance flat, Kissht an 11.7% premium. A strong public market helps late investors exit, which in time frees capital for early funds.

Source: The Runway, Oct 4, 2026.

The RDI deep tech fund pauses its front door

The Rs 1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation fund has stopped taking new applications. The Technology Development Board closed its call on September 30, citing administrative reasons, after committing Rs 2,192 crore of soft loans to 22 companies in April, including Agnikul, GalaxEye, QuNu Labs and IdeaForge. More than 300 applied. BIRAC, the second agency, has not disbursed yet, awaiting finance ministry clarity on tax liabilities.

The Indian Express reported that 15 of the 22 first-round recipients had investment ties to selection-committee members. On October 1 the government called the cash-crunch framing not correct and said the pause reflected a surge of proposals, and that more disbursing agencies would be appointed. The cause is disputed; the closed door is not.

Source: The Runway, summarising The Indian Express and government statements, Oct 4, 2026.
